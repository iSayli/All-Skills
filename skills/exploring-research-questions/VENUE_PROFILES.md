# Venue Profiles

Pre-built profiles for common target venues. Supplement with live search of recent issues.

## NeurIPS / ICML / ICLR
- **Contribution type**: New methods, new theory, empirical analysis of ML systems
- **RQ style**: "We propose X which achieves Y" or "We show that phenomenon Z exists in current models"
- **Theory**: Mathematical proofs, statistical learning theory, or strong empirical claims
- **Data**: Benchmark datasets, synthetic, or large-scale model outputs
- **What they reject**: Purely observational social science without methodological novelty

## Nature / Science / PNAS
- **Contribution type**: Broad scientific significance, novel empirical finding with societal implication
- **RQ style**: Clean causal or correlational claim with strong identification
- **Theory**: Should generalize; findings must matter beyond the specific dataset
- **Data**: Large-scale, often novel data collection; experiments preferred
- **What they reject**: Incremental findings, methods papers without clear empirical insight

## Management Science / Marketing Science
- **Contribution type**: Causal identification of firm/consumer behavior; theory + empirics
- **RQ style**: "Does X cause Y? Under what conditions?"
- **Theory**: Microeconomic, behavioral economics, or organizational theory
- **Data**: Field experiments, natural experiments, large transactional datasets
- **What they reject**: Descriptive work without causal mechanism, purely ML papers

## American Political Science Review (APSR) / AJPS
- **Contribution type**: New empirical finding about political behavior, institutions, or preferences
- **RQ style**: Causal or descriptive claims with clear theoretical motivation
- **Theory**: Rational choice, historical institutionalism, political psychology
- **Data**: Survey experiments, observational + matching, case studies + QCA
- **What they reject**: Atheoretical data analysis

## CHI / CSCW / WWW
- **Contribution type**: Understanding human behavior on/with technology; design implications
- **RQ style**: "How do users X?" or "What is the effect of feature Y on behavior Z?"
- **Theory**: Cognitive science, social psychology, HCI frameworks
- **Data**: Log data, surveys, lab experiments, field studies
- **What they reject**: Work without clear human-centered contribution

## Nature Human Behaviour
- **Contribution type**: Insights into human behavior with cross-disciplinary significance
- **RQ style**: Clean empirical finding with implications beyond one discipline
- **Theory**: Any — but must be explicit and load-bearing
- **Data**: Large-scale, ideally pre-registered; replication encouraged
- **What they reject**: Single-lab, low-N, or purely methodological work
