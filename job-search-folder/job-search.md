# Job Search Profile

This file defines WHAT I'm looking for. Related files, each with one job:

- `job-evaluation.md`: how to score and report roles
- `search-sources.md`: where to look
- `search-queries.md`: what to search (Cowork expands this over time)
- Job application tailor skill: my background, proof points, and how to pitch myself. Do not duplicate any of that here.

---

## Objective

Find full-time, permanent Product Design / UX Design roles where my existing experience gives me a credible reason to apply.

Quality over volume. A short list of strong matches beats a long list of weak ones. When unsure, include the role with a flag instead of dropping it silently, but keep the list short.

I am NOT looking for engineering roles despite my Computer Engineering background. Prototyping and building are a strength that supports design work, not the job itself.

---

## Target titles

**Strong matches**
- Product Designer / Product Designer II
- UX Designer / UX/UI Designer / UX Product Designer
- AI Product Designer / Product Designer, AI / Product Designer, AI Systems
- UX Designer, AI
- Product Designer, B2B SaaS / Enterprise / Fintech / Platform
- Founding Designer / First Designer (only if scope is product design)

**Judge by description**
- Interaction Designer
- Experience Designer
- UX Researcher / Designer hybrid
- Design Technologist, Design Engineer, UX Engineer (only if design-led, not primarily coding)
- Conversation / AI Experience Designer

**Do not prioritize:** roles where the main expectation is software engineering, visual/graphic production, marketing/web design, UX writing, or design operations.

---

## Experience level

- Sweet spot: 2-5 years, mid-level.
- Roles asking 1-3 years are fine if compensation is above ₹8 LPA (India) or clearly competitive for the market.
- Consider up to ~6-7 years if responsibilities are a strong match (AI, 0→1, complex workflows).
- Skip: internships, entry-level (<1 year), Senior / Staff / Principal / Lead / Manager unless unusually well matched.

---

## Employment type

- Prioritize: full-time, permanent, long-term.
- Exclude: unpaid, internships, freelance-only, contract-only, temporary.
- Contract-to-hire: flag, don't exclude.

---

## Compensation

- India full-time: floor of ₹8 LPA. If salary is not listed, keep the role and flag "salary unlisted".
- US: no fixed floor yet. Report the range if listed.

---

## Geography and work arrangement

**Primary: India**
- Strong preference: Mumbai, Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, Delhi NCR (Gurgaon, Noida)
- Also consider: Chennai, Remote India

**Secondary: United States**
- Work authorization matters. Flag every US role: `Sponsors visa? Yes / No / Unclear / Requires US work auth`.
- Deprioritize roles that state "no sponsorship".
- Remote-US only if they hire internationally or through an employer of record.

**Arrangement:** remote, hybrid and in-person are all acceptable.

## Companies and industries

**High priority**
- AI-native products and startups (agents, copilots, AI workflows, trust & safety, document intelligence)
- Fintech and financial services (lending, credit, risk, compliance, banking infra)
- B2B SaaS / enterprise workflow tools
- Edtech
- Early-stage startups, especially Y Combinator companies (Seed to Series B)

**Also consider**
- Established product companies with known product design teams
- Accessibility-focused products
- Journalism, civic tech, Trust & Safety products

**Lower priority:** agencies and consultancies (unless product-focused), large IT services firms, ad-tech / e-commerce roles that are mostly visual.

---

## Signals to score up

- 0→1 or early-stage product design, founding / first designer scope
- Complex workflows, B2B / enterprise, domain-heavy products
- AI/ML products, human-in-the-loop, AI transparency, agent or copilot UX
- Designer owns research; mixed-methods
- Design systems
- Prototyping, AI-assisted prototyping, design-engineering collaboration
- Close work with PM and engineering
- Data-informed design
- Accessibility
- Ambiguous problems, end-to-end ownership
- Regulated or compliance-heavy domains (KYC, AML, etc.)

## Signals to score down

- Primarily visual/UI production, graphic, marketing or web design
- Primarily UX writing or design ops
- Primarily engineering
- Requires substantially more experience, or is a lead/manager role
- Vague posting with no product detail
- Stale (older than ~45 days, or marked closed)
- Duplicate of another listing
- Salary clearly below the floor
